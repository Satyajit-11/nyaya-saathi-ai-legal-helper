**Which Indian law applies**  

- **Payment of Wages Act 1936** – requires wages to be paid on the scheduled date; non‑payment is a violation.  
- **Code on Wages 2019** – supersedes the 1936 Act in many states; also mandates timely payment of wages.  
- **Indian Contract Act 1872** – the employment relationship is a contract; breach (non‑payment of salary, wrongful deduction of notice‑period recovery) can be remedied in civil court.  

*State‑specific provisions such as the Maharashtra Shops & Establishments Act may also govern salary timelines, but I do not have enough specific statutory context for that exact clause; please consult a licensed advocate.*
